When water is spreading in Gambier

If water is spreading, treat it as an emergency, whether it is a pipe, an appliance or rain coming through a roof. Shut off the main water valve, switch off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and call us with a description of where the water is.

Gambier is compact, so help is close, but the first steps are yours. A few minutes spent closing a valve and lifting valuables can save a floor or a ceiling.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Stop the source

Close the main shutoff valve, or the valve at the fixture or appliance that is leaking.

Power and safety

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry. Call the utility or 911 for sparks or gas.

Lift valuables

Move furniture legs, rugs, books and electronics off wet floors.

Keep people and pets out

Stay out of contaminated or dark water, and keep children away from standing water.

A village built around a college and a river

Kenyon College was founded in 1824 alongside the village, and its campus and Middle Path shape the center of Gambier. The college grounds, the older homes around them and the small streets mean many buildings are decades old, though no local survey exists that we can cite on exact ages. In practical terms, ask when the plumbing and roof were last replaced, because that tells you more about leak risk than the street name.

The Kokosing River runs along the village, and the Kokosing Gap Trail crosses bridges over it. Where a house sits relative to the river and its small tributaries is worth checking on the FEMA flood map for your address, and the village office can tell you what applies to your lot.

Water service: who does what in Gambier

The Gambier Village Water System receives its drinking water from the City of Mount Vernon, which draws groundwater from municipal wells, and the village maintains the distribution lines. A main break in the street is a village matter, while a failed pipe inside your home is yours.

The village also has a wastewater ordinance, so if sewage backs up and the blockage seems to be in the main, contact the village. If it is in your own line, a plumber clears it, and we handle the contaminated water.

Places in Gambier that shape the calls we get

Homes near Wiggin Street, around Meadow Lane and near the Kenyon campus have different layouts, but the problems repeat. A hose fails in a laundry closet, a supply line splits behind a bathroom wall or a gutter overflows after a storm.

The public library branch and Wiggin Street Elementary are public buildings with their own facilities staff, so a loss there goes through them. For homes, rentals and small commercial spaces, we remove water and dry the structure, and the basement page and kitchen page explain two of the most common cases.

Renters, owners and landlords in Gambier

Many Gambier households rent from a landlord or a property owner. If you rent and water is spreading, shut off the water if you can, tell the owner right away and call us. Keep photos and the dates you reported the problem.

Ohio law, in Revised Code 5321.04, requires landlords to keep plumbing in good working order and keep the unit fit and habitable, so the owner is generally the one who arranges repairs. We document the water damage so the record is clear.
