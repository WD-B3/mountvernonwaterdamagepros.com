Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Overview

This site has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. The main way to reach us is by phone.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and a description of the water damage so we can arrange the response. We use it for that purpose and to improve how calls are handled.

Website data

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded map from Google Maps, and those services may collect information under their own policies.

Sharing

We share call details with the restoration professionals who respond to your request, and with others only as needed to handle the request or as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us not to record or contact you again by telling the person who answers. To ask about your information, email contact@mountvernonwaterdamagepros.com.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date above shows the latest version.
