Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Mount Vernon, OH

Category 3 water from a drain, toilet or main line: stay out and let trained crews handle it.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Emergency sewage backup cleanup in Mount Vernon, OH deals with Category 3 water, which carries bacteria and other contaminants, and the 24/7 line is open. Keep people and pets out of the affected room, do not use the plumbing, and do not try to clean it with towels or a household wet vac.

Sewage damage is different because the water itself is the hazard. Porous materials it touches, such as carpet, pad and drywall, are usually removed and discarded, and hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized. If the water came from a flooded basement floor drain, the basement page covers the room and the flood page covers storm-driven backups.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Safety first

  • Stay out of the water, and keep children and pets away.
  • Do not run water, flush toilets or use the washer, because that sends more into the house.
  • If water touches outlets or the panel, call the electric utility or 911 before you call us.
  • Wear no skin contact in the water, and do not use fans to dry it, since that spreads contamination.
  • Photograph the damage from a safe distance and call the 24/7 line.

Where backups come from

A backup can start in the house, from a blocked line, or outside, from an overloaded main. The City of Mount Vernon operates a wastewater collection system of about 219 miles of lines, and the city asks residents to keep fats, oils and grease out of drains. Grease and flushed items cause many blockages, and that is a prevention step any household can take.

If the blockage is in your own lateral, a plumber clears it. If the backup appears to come from the city main, contact the city utility, and keep the written record of what you report and when. In either case, we handle the contaminated water.

Why sewage water is Category 3

Under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, sewage and similar grossly contaminated water is classed as Category 3. That means carpet and pad that were wet are removed, and drywall wet by it is cut out, instead of being dried and kept.

The crew wears protective gear, removes contaminated materials in sealed bags, and uses extraction equipment before cleaning and applying disinfectant to surfaces left in place. Air movers dry the structure afterward.

What cannot be saved

  • Carpet, pad and upholstered items that soaked in the water.
  • Drywall, insulation and cabinet bases that wicked contaminated water.
  • Cardboard, paper and fabric items stored low.
  • Food, medication and anything in a container that touched the water.

What can be cleaned

Concrete, tile, sealed framing and many solid surfaces can be cleaned and sanitized in place. The crew tests moisture afterward and keeps equipment running until readings are dry. Because health questions belong to a physician, ask your doctor if anyone in the house is sick after exposure.

What affects the cost of sewage backup cleanup

Sewage jobs involve more labor and disposal than clean water jobs. These are the factors.

  • Area affected. More square footage means more removal and sanitizing.
  • Materials removed. Carpet, pad and drywall are discarded and replaced.
  • Contamination level. Heavily contaminated water needs more steps than a minor overflow.
  • Drying time. After cleaning, the structure is dried, and readings decide the days.
  • Source repair. Clearing or repairing the line is the plumber or the city.

Sewage backups and insurance

Sewer and drain backup is often excluded from a standard homeowners policy and covered by an endorsement. Read your policy or ask your insurer whether you have it, and keep photos and the plumber report.

Questions about sewage backup cleanup in Mount Vernon

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Mount Vernon?

Yes. Sewage backups happen at night and on weekends, and the line is answered 24/7. Stay out of the water, tell the dispatcher which room is affected and wait for guidance.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

It is not recommended. Contaminated water needs protective gear and proper disposal, and household cleaning will not make porous materials safe.

Who do I call, you or the city?

If it is a backup from your own lateral, a plumber clears it. If it appears to come from the main, contact the city utility. We remove and sanitize either way.

Is it safe to stay in the house?

That depends on how far the water spread and what it touched. Keep people and pets away from affected rooms, and ask your doctor about any symptoms.
