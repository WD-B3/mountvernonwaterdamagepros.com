Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Mount Vernon, OH

A pipe that froze and then thawed into a leak: what to do and what the cleanup involves.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Frozen pipe water damage in Mount Vernon, OH often appears when the weather warms, because a split pipe stays plugged with ice until it thaws and then leaks. The 24/7 line is open, and while you wait you should shut off the main water valve, open the faucet nearest the frozen section and keep heat on in the house.

This page is about the leak and the damage after a freeze. The source differs from a burst pipe from pressure or wear, because the pipe fails where ice expanded inside it, usually in an unheated or exposed spot such as an exterior wall, crawl space, attic or garage.

When a frozen pipe becomes an emergency

A pipe that is frozen but not leaking is a warning, and the best move is to shut off the water and thaw it gently. A pipe that is leaking or has water showing on a wall or ceiling is an emergency, especially as temperatures rise and the ice melts.

Never thaw a pipe with an open flame, and never use a heat source near wet materials. A hair dryer on low and warm towels are safer, and any sign of leaking means the water should be shut off at the main.

Why pipes split and where

Water expands as it freezes, and the pressure builds between the ice plug and the closed faucet, which is where the pipe splits. The split is usually not at the ice itself, so a pipe can look fine while it is damaged a few feet away.

Knox County winters bring freezing nights and thaws, and the cycle is hard on pipes in exterior walls, crawl spaces and garages. Homes with unheated additions or older insulation are the common trouble spots, though the exact local frequency is not something a company can state without a source, so the safest advice is to check your own exposed pipes before winter.

Symptoms after a freeze

  • Water stains or drips on a wall or ceiling after temperatures rise.
  • A sudden drop in water pressure or a faucet that runs weakly.
  • A hissing sound in a wall or crawl space.
  • Visible frost on a pipe in an unheated space.

Cleanup after the thaw

The crew extracts water, opens wet wall and ceiling cavities where needed, and dries with air movers and dehumidifiers. A thermal camera is useful because the leak path often runs inside an exterior wall where insulation holds water.

A plumber repairs the pipe, and the two trades are separate. If the water reached the crawl space or under-floor area, the crawl space page covers that void, and wall repair is covered on the wall and drywall page.

Prevention for next winter

Insulate pipes in unheated spaces, seal air leaks around them, let a faucet drip during a hard freeze and know where the main shutoff is. If you will be away, keep the heat set high enough to protect the plumbing.

What affects the cost of frozen pipe cleanup

These factors move the total after a freeze-related leak.

  • Where the pipe split. A split inside an exterior wall means opening the wall to dry and repair it.
  • How long it leaked. A leak that ran during a thaw while no one was home can wet several rooms.
  • Insulation and finishes. Wet insulation is removed, and finishes are replaced after drying.
  • Drying time. Cold exterior walls dry slowly, so equipment may run longer.
  • Number of pipes. More than one split multiplies the cavities to open and dry.

Frozen pipes and insurance

Freezing damage is often covered when it is sudden and accidental, but some policies expect the home to have been kept heated. Read your policy or ask your insurer about conditions, and document that you shut off the water promptly.

Questions about frozen pipe water damage in Mount Vernon

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Mount Vernon?

Yes. Freeze-related leaks tend to show up at odd hours when temperatures change, and the line is answered 24/7. Shut off the main and call us, and describe where the pipe runs.

Can I thaw the pipe myself?

You can warm a frozen section gently with a hair dryer on low or warm towels, with the water shut off at the main. Never use an open flame, and call a plumber if you cannot reach the frozen section.

Will the pipe leak right away?

Not always. A split pipe often stays blocked by ice and leaks only as it thaws, so a house can be fine at night and wet by noon.

Is this covered by my home insurance?

It often can be when it is sudden and accidental, but policies differ on conditions such as heat in the home. Ask your insurer about your own policy.
