Water Damage Services in Mount Vernon, OH

Twenty services grouped by cause, so you can find the page that matches what you are seeing.

☎ Call (740) 231-9132

Water damage services are easiest to choose by the source of the water and the material it reached. The groups below follow that order, and each page explains the emergency steps, the process and what affects the cost. If you are not sure which fits, call the line and describe what you see.

Pipes and plumbing

Pressurized lines, frozen pipes, tanks and under-slab leaks, each with its own shutoff and cleanup.

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A pressurized supply line failed: shut-off steps, extraction and drying.

See burst pipe water damage

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

A pipe that froze, split and leaked as it thawed, with extraction and drying to follow.

See frozen pipe water damage

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

A failed tank or leaking fitting, with the water extracted and the surrounding area dried.

See water heater leak cleanup

Slab Leak Water Damage

A supply line under a concrete slab that leaks, with drying and a plumber for the repair.

See slab leak water damage

Appliances and HVAC

Hoses, drain lines and condensate that leak small amounts for a long time.

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washers, dishwashers, refrigerators and ice makers: supply or drain failures and the water they leave.

See appliance leak water damage

AC Leak Water Damage

Clogged condensate lines, overflowing pans and air handlers that leaked into ceilings and walls.

See ac leak water damage

Kitchen Water Damage

Swollen cabinets, leaks under the sink and wet floors around the kitchen.

See kitchen water damage

Weather and building envelope

Roofs, storms, rising water, below-grade rooms and the crawl space under the house.

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Stains, sagging drywall and roof leaks, whatever the source above the ceiling.

See ceiling water damage repair

Storm Water Damage

Wind, rain and storm-event damage through the roof, windows and siding.

See storm water damage

Flood Damage Cleanup

River, creek and groundwater flooding, including the contaminated water that comes with it.

See flood damage cleanup

Basement Water Damage

The below-grade room: sump failures, seepage and finished basements that took water.

See basement water damage

Crawl Space Water Damage

The under-floor void: standing water, wet insulation, damp joists and the moisture that rises into the house.

See crawl space water damage

Water removal and drying

The core of mitigation: pumping water out, drying the structure and handling contaminated water safely.

Water Extraction

Standing water removed right now with pumps and extraction units, before it soaks the structure.

See water extraction

Structural Drying

See structural drying

Sewage Backup Cleanup

See sewage backup cleanup

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Cupping, buckling and staining in wood and laminate flooring, and when drying saves it.

Carpet Water Damage

Wet carpet and pad: extraction, drying and when replacement is the safer call.

See carpet water damage

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Wet drywall, baseboards and wall cavities: when walls can be dried instead of replaced.
